Who We Are

About Dimino Physical Therapy

Dimino Physical Therapy provides personalized physiotherapy care in Staten Island. The clinic focuses on rehabilitation, pain reduction, and restoring movement using evidence-based physical therapy treatments. The goal is to help patients recover mobility and return to normal daily activities safely.

  • Licensed physical therapy professionals
  • Personalized rehabilitation programs
  • Injury recovery and pain management
  • Mobility and strengthening therapy
